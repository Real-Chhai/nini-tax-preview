# Nini Tax — Designer's recommended version (warm practice)

This is the **recommended** look: a warm private practice, not a mashup of navy / studio / black-gold / cream.

Someone just got an IRS letter. They need a licensed person, in Khmer or English, today. The site should feel like that person — warm, clear, credible. Not a nightclub, not a government portal, not a fashion stationery shop.

Gold is metal for the call button, not a theme. Proof is the face, the EA badge, the IRS directory link, and a giant phone number.

## Look

Page `#E4D9C8` (a little darker, warm taupe), surfaces `#F0E6D8`, warm-black ink `#1A1410`. Dark warm nav `#241C16`. Gold `#E0A81A` is used only on the NT mark, the EA badge, italic *this is fixable*, the primary call control, and one short rule. Footer is warm black with cream type.

The portrait sits on the page as a photograph — no gold frame, no navy/black slab. Caption underneath in small caps.

Fonts are the point of this version:

- **Newsreader** — headlines (italic for emphasis). Literary, not luxury-hotel Playfair, not lawyer Baskerville, not Fraunces.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** — UI and body (17–18px). Designed for actual reading.
- **Kantumruy Pro** — Khmer.

Header lockup is warm-black **NT** on a gold square, plus “Nini Tax” in Atkinson (not letterspaced luxury caps). `favicon.svg` matches.

## Deploy

Unzip `nini-tax-practice.zip` into `public_html` (or any static host). The zip contains site files at the root — `index.html` should sit next to `owner.png`, not inside a nested `practice/` folder.

    unzip nini-tax-practice.zip -d public_html

Do not unzip this over the navy, studio, or black-gold packages unless you mean to replace them.
