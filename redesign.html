Questions

Things people ask before they call.

I just got a letter from the IRS. What should I do first?

Don’t ignore it, and don’t pay it just because it arrived. Most notices have a response deadline. Send me the notice number — top right, something like CP2000 or CP504 — and I’ll tell you what it means and what your options are.

What is an Enrolled Agent, and why does it matter?

An Enrolled Agent is licensed directly by the IRS after a three-part exam covering individual tax, business tax, and representation. It’s valid in all fifty states.

Only Enrolled Agents, CPAs, and attorneys can represent you before the IRS without limits. Most preparers can file a return but cannot argue your case.

I haven’t filed in years. Am I in serious trouble?

Usually less than you think. When you don’t file, the IRS can file for you using all of your income and none of your deductions — often a balance far higher than what’s really owed. Filing the real returns frequently reduces it substantially, and sometimes eliminates it.

Can you help if I don’t live in California?

Yes. The Enrolled Agent credential is federal. I work with clients across the country by phone, video, and secure portal.

Will I have to talk to the IRS myself?

No. Once you sign a power of attorney, I become the point of contact. Their correspondence comes to me, I make the calls, and I attend the audit if there is one.

How much will it cost?

Preparation prices are published above. Relief work is a flat fee after a free transcript review. You’ll know the full price before any work begins, and it doesn’t change afterward.

How do I know my information is safe?

Documents move through an encrypted TaxDome client portal, never email. W-2s, IDs, and anything with your Social Security number stay out of your inbox.

Engagement letters, Form 8879, and the power of attorney are signed electronically — no printing or driving required.

I keep a Written Information Security Plan on file as the IRS requires of paid preparers. Records stay encrypted in storage and in transit, and past returns stay available in your portal year round.

What do you need from me to get started?

For a free review: any notices you’ve received and permission to pull your IRS transcripts.
